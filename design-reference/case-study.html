CASE STUDY · MERCEDES-BENZ R&D INDIA HACKATHON

Stopping one hot cell from taking the pack with it.

In a battery pack, one overheating cell can heat its neighbour until that one fails too, and the next, and the next. My project is a passive barrier that breaks the chain, with no electronics and no power.

01 · THE PROBLEM

One cell fails, the pack follows.

Cells sit tightly packed in a module. Heat from one failing cell can spread to its neighbours, which is how a single fault becomes a pack fire.

02 · THE IDEA

A barrier that needs no power.

A passive barrier between cells that blocks the spread of heat. Nothing to switch on, nothing to fail electrically.

03 · THE PROOF

Tested without lithium.

A heated test rig stands in for a failing cell, so the barrier can be tested safely, without real lithium cells.
