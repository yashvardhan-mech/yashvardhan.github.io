Ready when you are

  1. Close the Serial Monitor in the Arduino IDE (or close the IDE).
  2. Plug in the Arduino, click Connect Arduino and pick its port (for example COM4).
  3. Switch on the 12 V supply. Connecting restarts the Arduino, so the run starts fresh: it waits for both blocks to be below 45 °C, then counts down.
